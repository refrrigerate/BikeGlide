Rental Terms

  1. Riders must be 18+ and carry a valid driving licence at pickup.
  2. Helmets must be worn at all times. One is provided free.
  3. Fuel is not included. Return the bike with the same fuel level.
  4. A refundable ₹500 security deposit is collected per booking.
  5. Upcoming bookings can be cancelled free of charge.

Privacy

Your details are only used to manage your account and bookings. In this demo, all data stays in your own browser.
