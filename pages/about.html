BikeGlide was built with one simple idea: getting around Indian cities should be easy, affordable, and enjoyable. Whether you're exploring Goa's coastline on a weekend, commuting through Chandigarh, or embarking on a Himalayan road trip — we want the right bike to always be within reach.

Our Mission

To democratise two-wheel mobility across India by providing a reliable, transparent, and rider-first bike rental experience — from nimble Italian scooters to superbikes, cruisers and adventure tourers.

We believe every journey deserves a well-maintained bike, fair pricing, and the peace of mind that comes from knowing help is always nearby.

  • Safety First

    Every bike passes a 20-point safety inspection before each rental. No exceptions.

  • Transparent Pricing

    What you see is what you pay. No hidden fees, no surprise charges at pickup.

  • Rider First

    Every decision we make — from bike selection to insurance — is made with the rider in mind.

Our Commitment

BikeGlide partners only with trusted bike owners and authorised dealers. Every vehicle on our platform is verified, insured, and serviced regularly.

We provide comprehensive rental insurance on every booking so that riders can focus on the road, not the what-ifs.

Our 24/7 roadside assistance team covers all major routes across our operational cities. If you run into trouble, one call is all it takes.

Why BikeGlide?

Unlike traditional bike rentals, BikeGlide is designed around the digital rider. Our platform makes it possible to browse, compare, and book a bike in minutes — with real-time availability, verified listings, and instant confirmation.

We cover everyday commutes, city tourism, and multi-day road trips with the same level of care. A college student in Chandigarh and a tourist in Goa both deserve a clean, well-maintained ride.

Safety First

All riders are encouraged to wear helmets at all times. We provide complimentary helmets with every booking.

Our bikes are fitted with working headlights, indicators, and mirrors as verified during the pre-rental checklist.

Riders must hold a valid driving license for the category of vehicle they rent. License verification happens at pickup.

Our Vision

By 2028, we aim to be present in 50+ cities, offer electric vehicle options, and power a connected ecosystem where bike owners, riders, and service providers come together on a single trusted platform.
